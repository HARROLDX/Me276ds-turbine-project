燃气轮机 NOₓ 软测量
Estimating Gas Turbine NOₓ from Operating Data

Jiawei Xia · Rongqian Zhang · Bowen Xie · Zhuoqun Yang

当运行人员发现 NOₓ 读数异常时，需要判断它是否与工况变化一致。用常规读数估计同期 NOₓ，可以为排查提供第二个信号。未来可用作排放监测的辅助工具，帮助安排复查或排查传感器故障。

An unexpected NOₓ reading prompts an operator to check whether it tracks a change in operating conditions. A same-hour estimate from routine sensors provides a second signal for prioritizing checks and investigating possible sensor faults.

选择 UCI 数据，因为它同时记录排放实测值和环境、机组读数，且保留时间顺序。数据来自土耳其西北部同一电厂，2011–2015 年共 36,733 条小时聚合记录。九个输入是 AT、AP、AH、AFDP、GTEP、TIT、TAT、TEY、CDP，目标是 NOₓ 浓度（mg/m³）。排除 CO、年份和行号。

The UCI data pair measured emissions with ambient and operating readings in chronological order. They contain 36,733 hourly aggregates from one plant in northwestern Türkiye over 2011–2015. Nine readings are inputs. NOₓ concentration (mg/m³) is the target. CO, year and row identifier are excluded.

分析顺序为训练窗口 → MLP 结构 → 单输入消融 → L2 强度。全部在 2015 后半段原始顺序的 3,692 条记录上测试。日期未随记录发布，因此这里按行数切半。

The study progresses from training window to MLP architecture, one-input ablation and L2 strength. All configurations are tested on the same 3,692 records from the second half of ordered 2015 rows. The split is by row count because exact timestamps are unavailable.

主实验 · 方法与评价
Main experiment · Methods and evaluation

普通最小二乘使用 ŷ = b + wᵀx，最小化残差平方和。输入标准化仅拟合训练行。

OLS estimates ŷ = b + wᵀx by minimizing squared residuals. Input scaling is fitted on training rows.

随机森林对 200 棵最大深度 12 的回归树取平均。每棵树使用自助抽样，预测为叶节点的均值。

Random forest averages 200 bootstrap regression trees of maximum depth 12. A tree predicts the mean target in the reached leaf.

梯度提升从训练目标均值出发，逐轮加上拟合残差的新树 Fₘ = Fₘ₋₁ + 0.05hₘ。使用 100 棵最大深度 3 的树。

Gradient boosting starts at the training target mean and adds trees fitted to residuals, Fₘ = Fₘ₋₁ + 0.05hₘ. It uses 100 trees of maximum depth 3.

MLP 基线为 9 → 32（ReLU）→ 1。每层先计算 Wx+b，隐藏层应用 max(0,z)。输入与目标均按训练集标准化，输出反变换为 mg/m³。Adam 初始学习率 0.001，最多 500 轮，α=0.01，随机种子 276。

The baseline MLP is 9 → 32 (ReLU) → 1. Each layer computes Wx+b, with max(0,z) in the hidden layer. Inputs and targets are scaled using training statistics and predictions return to mg/m³. Adam starts at 0.001 with at most 500 epochs, α=0.01 and seed 276.

MAE = mean(|y−ŷ|)，RMSE = √mean((y−ŷ)²)，两者单位为 mg/m³。R² = 1 − SSE/SST，其中 SST 围绕测试均值计算。负 R² 表示平方误差超过这个均值参照。各阶段按最低测试 RMSE 选配置。

MAE and RMSE are in mg/m³. R² = 1 − SSE/SST, with SST computed around the test mean. Negative R² indicates squared error above that reference. Each stage ranks candidates by minimum test RMSE.

最后阶段最低 RMSE 为 7.020，对应 α=0.0，MAE=5.622，R²=0.352。四阶段追踪增加历史数据、调整网络、精简输入与约束权重是否改善同批后期记录的表现。

The final-stage minimum RMSE is 7.020 at α=0.0, with MAE=5.622 and R²=0.352. The four stages trace how later-record performance changes with historical coverage, architecture, input removal and weight penalties.

各阶段反复使用同一测试集选择设置，分数属于探索性比较。单电厂数据、固定种子和缺少独立新时段，限制了泛化判断。若投入实际监测，应冻结选出的方案，在新时间段测试，再评估跨机组可迁移性和报警阈值。

Selection reuses the same test set across stages, making scores exploratory. A single plant, fixed seed and lack of a fresh period limit generalization claims. For monitoring use, freeze the selected settings, evaluate a new period, and then assess transfer across turbines and alarm thresholds.
